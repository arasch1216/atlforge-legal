# Privacy — paste into Squarespace

**Last updated:** 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **AD Lab Development** handles information in connection with the Atlassian Forge app **Ticket Quality Auditor** (the “App”).

## 1. Who this applies to

Organizations that install the App on Atlassian Cloud (Jira) and users of those installations.

## 2. Data we access

Through Atlassian Forge APIs, the App may access Jira issue fields used for quality analysis, app configuration, and derived analysis results (scores, findings, recommendations). We access this data only to provide App functionality inside the customer’s Atlassian site.

## 3. Data we store

Configuration and analysis results are stored in **Atlassian Forge app storage (KVS)**, scoped to the customer’s installation. We do not operate a separate customer database outside Atlassian.

## 4. Artificial intelligence processing

When AI is enabled, selected ticket text may be processed by **Atlassian-hosted Forge LLMs**. When AI is disabled, analysis is deterministic only.

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not sell customer Jira content
- We do not use customer ticket content to train public third-party models
- We do not collect payment card details (Marketplace billing is handled by Atlassian when paid)

## 6. Retention & deletion

Data is retained until overwritten, invalidated, or removed on uninstall per Atlassian platform behavior. Contact us for deletion requests.
