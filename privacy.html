Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AD Sycronicity (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Atlassian Forge app Jira Ticket Quality Auditor (the “App”).

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to organizations that install the App on Atlassian Cloud (Jira) and to users of those installations.

2. Data we access

Through Atlassian Forge APIs, the App may access:

  • Jira issue fields used for quality analysis (for example: summary, description, issue type, priority, labels, components, linked issues, project, and timestamps)
  • App configuration set by administrators
  • Derived analysis results (scores, findings, recommendations)

We access this data only to provide App functionality inside the customer’s Atlassian site.

3. Data we store

The App stores configuration and derived analysis results in Atlassian Forge app storage (KVS), scoped to the customer’s installation. We do not operate a separate customer database outside Atlassian for core App state.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

When AI features are enabled, selected ticket text may be processed by Atlassian-hosted Forge LLMs to produce structured quality assessments and optional rewrite suggestions. This processing is designed to remain within the Atlassian trust boundary (Runs on Atlassian–oriented architecture).

When AI is disabled, analysis is deterministic (rules/scoring only) and no LLM calls are made.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell customer Jira content
  • We do not use customer ticket content to train public third-party models outside the Forge LLM platform path described above
  • We do not require customers to send payment card details to us (Marketplace billing is handled by Atlassian when the App is paid)

6. Retention

Stored analysis is retained until overwritten by a newer analysis, invalidated by configuration/product logic, or removed as part of app uninstall / Atlassian platform retention behavior for Forge storage.

7. Deletion / uninstall

Uninstalling the App removes the App’s access to the site. Forge-hosted storage retention and recovery follow Atlassian platform behavior. For deletion requests, contact us using the email below.

8. Subprocessors / platform providers

The App runs on Atlassian Forge. AI features use Atlassian Forge LLMs. Atlassian’s platform terms and privacy documentation also apply to underlying hosting.

9. Customer responsibilities

  • Configure the App appropriately for your organization
  • Ensure your use of Jira content with the App complies with your internal policies and applicable law
  • Provide notices to your users as required for your jurisdiction
