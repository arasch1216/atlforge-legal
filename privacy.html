Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AD Lab Development (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Atlassian Forge app Ticket Quality Auditor (the “App”).

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to organizations that install the App on Atlassian Cloud (Jira) and to users of those installations.

2. Data we access

Through Atlassian Forge APIs, the App may access:

  • Jira issue fields used for quality analysis (summary, description, issue type, priority, labels, components, linked issues, project, timestamps)
  • App configuration set by administrators
  • Derived analysis results (scores, findings, recommendations)

We access this data only to provide App functionality inside the customer’s Atlassian site.

3. Data we store

The App stores configuration and derived analysis results in Atlassian Forge app storage (KVS), scoped to the customer’s installation. We do not operate a separate customer database outside Atlassian for core App state.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

When AI features are enabled, selected ticket text may be processed by Atlassian-hosted Forge LLMs to produce structured quality assessments and optional rewrite suggestions. This processing remains within the Atlassian trust boundary.

When AI is disabled, analysis is deterministic (rules/scoring only) and no LLM calls are made.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell customer Jira content
  • We do not use customer ticket content to train public third-party models
  • We do not collect payment card details (Marketplace billing is handled by Atlassian when the App is paid)

6. Retention

Stored analysis is retained until overwritten by a newer analysis, invalidated by configuration logic, or removed as part of app uninstall / Atlassian platform retention.

7. Deletion / uninstall

Uninstalling the App removes the App’s access to the site. For deletion requests, contact us using the email below.

8. Subprocessors / platform providers

The App runs on Atlassian Forge. AI features use Atlassian Forge LLMs. Atlassian’s platform terms and privacy documentation also apply.

9. Customer responsibilities

  • Configure the App appropriately for your organization
  • Ensure your use of Jira content complies with your internal policies and applicable law
  • Provide notices to your users as required for your jurisdiction
